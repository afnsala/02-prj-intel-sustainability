# Intel: Sustainability Through the Ages

An interactive, single-page timeline that tells the story of Intel's history and sustainability milestones, from its founding in 1968 to its 2024 Sustainability Summit.

## Features

- **Flip-card timeline** — each milestone card flips (via click, tap, or keyboard) to reveal more detail, with background imagery for every era
- **Company history + sustainability milestones** — covers foundational moments (Intel's founding, first microprocessor, 8086 and 386 processors) alongside sustainability milestones (peak GHG emissions, the RISE strategy, net-zero commitment, renewable electricity achievements, and the first Sustainability Summit)
- **"Our Commitments" section** — three highlighted pillar cards covering the RISE strategy, net-zero commitment, and water & waste initiatives
- **Newsletter signup form** — with name, email, and topic-of-interest checkboxes
- **Accessibility built in** — skip-to-content link, keyboard-navigable timeline cards, ARIA labeling, and visually-hidden headings for screen readers
- **Automatic RTL support** — `rtl-detect.js` detects language/direction changes (e.g. from Google Translate) and applies right-to-left layout automatically
- **Responsive design** — adapts across desktop and mobile viewports

## Built With

- HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript
- [Bootstrap 5](https://getbootstrap.com/) & [Bootstrap Icons](https://icons.getbootstrap.com/)
- Google Fonts (Rajdhani & Inter)
